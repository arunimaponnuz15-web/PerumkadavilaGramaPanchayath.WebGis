About Our Panchayat

Perumkadavila Grama Panchayat is a local self-government institution in Kerala. Grama Panchayats play an important role in addressing local needs, supporting community development and facilitating essential civic services.

Local governance encourages public participation in development planning and helps address community priorities. Residents can contribute by staying informed, participating in local activities and using the appropriate official channels for public services.

This website is intended to make general Panchayat-related information easier to access in one place.

Development and Environment

Sustainable Panchayat development involves improving infrastructure, supporting community well-being and protecting natural resources. Public participation is important in identifying local needs and priorities.

Infrastructure

Roads, drainage and public facilities support accessibility, safety and everyday community needs.

Waste Management

Waste segregation, recycling and responsible disposal help reduce pollution and maintain clean neighbourhoods.

Water and Drainage

Protecting water resources and keeping drainage channels clear can support a healthier environment and help reduce local flood risks.

Community Participation

Residents can support local development through public meetings, community programmes and environmental awareness activities.
